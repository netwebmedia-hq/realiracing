Five rules for any deal

  1. Name the exact model and variant before you open the listing, and match it letter for letter on the day.
  2. Compare the price with its history on a retailer you trust, not with the crossed-out price.
  3. Write down what must be in the box: rim, power supply, quick release, cables, mounting hardware.
  4. Confirm the part works with the base, pedals and frame you already own.
  5. Read the return window, who pays return freight, and whether the clock starts at purchase or at delivery.
Do this before the sales start

Make a one-page list this week with two columns: what your rig needs this season, and what you would only like to have. Buy from the first column when a fair price appears, and let the second column wait for a price you have already seen.

FAQ

When is Black Friday in 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026. Cyber Monday follows on Monday, November 30, 2026. Individual retailers set their own start and end dates.

Are there confirmed sim racing deals for Black Friday 2026?

Not on this page. As of October 20, 2026 RealIRacing has not verified any offer from a brand or retailer, so the guides here focus on how to judge a deal when it appears.

What should I buy first for iRacing on Black Friday?

Start with whatever limits your driving now. For many drivers that is a load cell brake or a stiffer seating position before a stronger wheelbase. The wait-for guide and the pedals and cockpit checklist cover the order.

How do I know a Black Friday price is real?

Compare it with the price history of the exact listing on a retailer you trust, and ignore the crossed-out price. Check the same model at one other retailer before you buy.
