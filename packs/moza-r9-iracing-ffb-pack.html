FAQ

Is this different from the free MOZA R9 settings post?

The free post gives the headline table. The pack adds the procedure and order, class-by-class guidance, the diagnosis table, the reasoning for each value and a log worksheet. If the post already solved it for you, you do not need the pack.

Does it include a Pit House file I can import?

No. Pit House preset files are not something I redistribute. You enter the values yourself (about ten minutes) and save your own preset. The pack includes a plain worksheet, not an import file.

Will it work on other bases?

The method will. The numbers are for a 9 N-m direct drive base, so treat them as starting points elsewhere.
