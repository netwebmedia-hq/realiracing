FAQ

Does it need admin rights?

No. A few machine-wide startup entries need admin to change; PitStop lists them and you turn them off in Task Manager.

Can I undo it?

Yes. Every startup change is logged and pitstop-startup.ps1 -Undo puts it all back. Priorities reset with -Restore.

Will it raise my frame rate?

No promises. It removes background load and stream interruptions; your FPS depends on your hardware and sim settings.
