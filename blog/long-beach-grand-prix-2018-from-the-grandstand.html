What happened

Alexander Rossi won the IndyCar race from pole in the blue and yellow NAPA car. He led 71 of the 85 laps.

The weekend was not only IndyCar. Stadium Super Trucks, the off-road truck series founded by Robby Gordon, ran on the same streets.

I watched from two spots. The grandstand gave me the front straight, and the fence put me close to the cars as they went past. Both angles are in the clips.

What the clips show

  • IndyCars under the Toyota arch.
  • The front straight, filmed from the stands.
  • Cars seen through the fence, close up.
  • Stadium Super Trucks on the street course.
  • A walk around the Mothers Exotic Car Paddock, with a Pagani Huayra BC, a Batman-style Lamborghini, Porsche 911s and rows of supercars.
  • A selfie of me, taken mid-race.

The Pagani Huayra BC is my most viewed clip from this weekend.
