Monsters on the Beach is a monster truck show run on the sand at the Virginia Beach oceanfront in Virginia, USA. The 2011 event ran May 6 to 8, and this page puts my clips from it in one place.

I filmed all of it myself, from the fence.

What happened

The course was built right on the beach, with sand ramps and a row of junk cars for the trucks to jump over.

The trucks announced for 2011 were Grave Digger, Stone Crusher, Mopar Magic, Gun Slinger, Tailgator, Bad Habit, El Toro Loco and Megasaurus. My clips don't cover all of them.

Scott Hartsock won the 2011 event, according to King Shocks.

What the clips show

  • Grave Digger jumping and throwing sand at the front row.
  • Stone Crusher right up against the fence.
  • Stone Crusher going over on its side after a big jump.
  • A red truck high over the crushed cars.
  • An orange truck with teal lettering.
  • A blue truck nose up over the ocean.

The thing to notice is the fence. Spectators stand at a chain-link fence very close to the track. That is where I stood to film, and it is why some clips have sand coming straight at the lens.

I don't know the names of the red, orange and blue trucks. If you do, tell me on the YouTube channel.
