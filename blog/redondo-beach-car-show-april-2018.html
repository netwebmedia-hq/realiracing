What the clips show

  • A rusty brown rat rod with a bare engine, chrome exhaust pipes out of the side, and whitewall tires on red wheels. A rat rod is a hot rod left rough on purpose.
  • A red Dodge Viper with the top down. The Viper was built with a V10 engine of 8 litres or more.
  • A silver classic Corvette with the hood up, in a row of cars with their hoods open. This third-generation Corvette was built from 1968 to 1982.
  • A red 1940s Ford, a green Porsche 911 and a red Chevelle SS with its hood up, all in one row.
  • A pale yellow Chevrolet Chevelle next to a gray modern Corvette with its hood up. Then a silver Spyder with 130 on its nose.
  • Sunset over the lot, with palm trees, a pale yellow old pickup and people walking between the cars.
