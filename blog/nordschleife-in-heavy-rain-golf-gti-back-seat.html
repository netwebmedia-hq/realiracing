What happened

The Nordschleife is the 20.8 km (12.9 mile) northern loop of the Nürburgring, built in the 1920s. People call it the Green Hell.

Most of the clips come from long onboard videos on my channel, cut down so you can watch the parts you care about. A few are from my phone on the dash, and some show the car park and the sim rigs at the track that week.

What the clips show

  • Wipers on, working against the rain.
  • Wet kerbs along the edge of the track.
  • Spray from the cars ahead.
  • Green verges and trees all around.
  • Short comparisons that put the real Döttinger Höhe and the real Karussell next to iRacing.

The comparison clips put the real place and the sim side by side, so you can look at the same spot twice.

Take it to the sim

iRacing also has the Nordschleife, so you can drive it yourself after watching the rain footage. To start on the Nürburgring in the sim, my Nürburgring GP iRacing guide is a good place to begin.
