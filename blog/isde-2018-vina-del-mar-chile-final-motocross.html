What happened

The 93rd ISDE ran from November 12 to 17, 2018. The first five days were enduro, and the last day was a motocross near Viña del Mar.

On the team side, Australia won the FIM World Trophy, the USA finished second and Italy third.

What the clips show

  • Riders jumping and roosting through a berm.
  • A rider right behind the fence.
  • Riders in the air over the big dirt jump.
  • The gate at the finish.
  • The whole track from a hill.
  • An enduro mass start, replayed at 1/4 speed.

Several clips repeat the action at 1/4 speed, so you can watch each moment a second time, slowly.
