What happened

The sprint ran on Saturday, July 26, and Max Verstappen won it.

Race day was Sunday, July 27. Rain held the start for about 80 minutes, then Oscar Piastri won the race.

What the clips show

  • F1 cars through La Source.
  • The valley around Eau Rouge and Raidillon, with its red and yellow kerbs.
  • Two McLarens.
  • A Kick Sauber and an Aston Martin below my seat.
  • Several passes replayed at 1/4 speed.
  • Splits that compare the real Eau Rouge with iRacing.

One clip needs a note. Spa-Francorchamps From the Grandstand is my most viewed Short of any kind, with about 30,000 views. It is a raw 13-second view from the stands, filmed on Sunday before the F1 start.
