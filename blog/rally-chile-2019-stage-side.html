I watched from the side of the stages near Concepción, Biobío, Chile, and filmed on my phone. Many of the clips replay the pass at 1/4 speed.

What happened

It was round 6 of 14 in the 2019 World Rally Championship.

Ott Tänak and co-driver Martin Järveoja won in a Toyota Yaris WRC.

What the clips show

  • A white car and a green car passing very close to where I stood.
  • A Hyundai WRC car sideways on gravel.
  • Cars running through the pines.
  • Spectators at the stage.
  • A Škoda rally driver up close.

Slowing a pass to 1/4 speed gives you time to see how a car sits sideways on gravel. A pass that is over in a blink becomes something you can study.
