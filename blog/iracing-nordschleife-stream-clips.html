What the clips show

  • Starts: the lights turn green on the BMW M4 GT4 EVO start straight, and a rolling start behind a pale-grey Porsche GT3 car.
  • Following cars: an orange-and-blue McLaren GT4 under the Bilstein gantry, and a red Porsche GT3 car through the kerbs and armco.
  • Close racing in the forest: a blue, white and orange Porsche GT3 car and a lime-green car run close together.
  • Rain from a Porsche cockpit: mist over the forest, wet corners, yellow tents and grey mist over the grandstands.
  • Quiet onboard runs in the BMW M4 GT4 EVO through the forest sections with the guardrail close.
  • Down the pit lane at the pit speed limit behind a yellow car, with the grandstands and a Mercedes logo ahead.
  • Sim rigs at the real Nürburgring, and the onboard replay of a lap on the TV that night.

About the Nordschleife

The Nordschleife is the old north loop of the Nurburgring in Germany. Its lap is 20.832 km. The Nürburgring also has a 5.1 km Grand Prix track. I also filmed the real place from a car in heavy rain: the Nordschleife in heavy rain.
