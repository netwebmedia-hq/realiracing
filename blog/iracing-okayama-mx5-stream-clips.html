What the clips show

  • A race start in the MX-5s. The lights go from red to green with a yellow MX-5 on the front, and a blue car spins in smoke on the right.
  • Door to door in an MX-5. A red MX-5 is ahead, and another MX-5 in Mazda livery runs right alongside.
  • Three white and blue MX-5s nose to tail through a corner and over the kerbs, seen from the replay camera.
  • A full field of MX-5s bunched up after the start, then a white MX-5 and a blue MX-5 side by side.
  • A lap from a Porsche cockpit past the wooded hills and the Lexus banner, with a car ahead on the straight.
  • A replay of a green Porsche GT3 car going through a corner, after a lap past the Autobacs banners and the grandstand.
  • Quiet laps from the Porsche cockpit past the tyre walls, the Pirelli and Autobacs banners and the wooded hills.

About Okayama

Okayama International Circuit is in Japan. One lap is 3.703 km. It hosted the Formula 1 Pacific Grand Prix in 1994 and 1995. More sim racing guides are on the blog.
