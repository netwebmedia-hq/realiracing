GT3 vs GT4 in iRacing — Which Class Should You Race First?

If you are wondering whether to start in GT3 or GT4, my recommendation is GT4 first, and it's not close. For a car to look at, see the BMW M4 GT4 guide. This is why, and when GT3's extra performance is actually worth the harder learning curve.

What actually separates the two classes

GT3 cars carry meaningfully more downforce and power than GT4 cars, which sounds like a simple upgrade but changes the driving problem itself. Aero-dependent grip means your confidence window shifts with speed in a way GT4's more mechanical-grip-driven balance doesn't. Learning car control fundamentals like trail braking, weight transfer, and throttle application on exit is a cleaner lesson in a GT4 car, where the grip you feel is closer to the grip you actually have at any given moment.

Why GT4 teaches better habits, faster

  • Lower overall grip means mistakes are more forgiving; you get more warning before you actually lose the car.
  • Less aero dependency means your braking points feel more consistent across a stint, which builds accurate instincts faster
  • A smaller performance gap between good and great drivers in GT4 means racecraft and consistency matter more relative to outright pace, so good early rating-building habits are essential.

When GT3 is the right move

Once GT4's habits become second nature, you brake late and consistently without being surprised when the car loses grip; your clean racecraft in traffic means GT3's extra performance becomes a genuine step forward instead of a trap. The aero-dependent confidence window that makes GT3 harder to learn on is exactly what makes it rewarding once you've built the fundamentals to handle it.

The honest test

If you can consistently finish clean races in GT4 without incidents costing you positions, you're ready to try GT3. If GT4 still occasionally surprises you, more reps there will pay off faster than jumping up early.

My take on where to start

The racing is close, the learning curve rewards fundamentals worth keeping sharp, and the field strength tends to be excellent. Start there. Move up once GT4 stops teaching you anything new, not before.

FAQ

Should I race GT3 or GT4 first on iRacing?

GT4 first, for almost everyone. Its lower downforce and more mechanical grip balance teach fundamental car control habits like trail braking, weight transfer, and consistent braking points more cleanly than GT3's aero-dependent grip window does.

What's the main difference between GT3 and GT4 cars in iRacing?

GT3 cars carry meaningfully more downforce and power, which shifts your grip and confidence window with speed. GT4 cars rely more on mechanical grip, giving more consistent, predictable feedback that's easier to learn fundamentals on.

How do I know I'm ready to move up from GT4 to GT3?

When you can consistently finish GT4 races clean, without incidents costing you positions, and the car rarely surprises you anymore, that is the signal GT4 has taught you what it can, and GT3's extra performance becomes rewarding instead of a trap.

Is GT4 a good class for building iRating?

Yes. GT4's smaller performance gap between good and great drivers rewards racecraft and consistency relative to outright pace, which builds exactly the clean-finish habits that grow iRating fastest early on.
