What the clips show

  • A GT3 pack in the wet, with a Porsche, a red Ferrari and a yellow-green Lamborghini.
  • A car runs off the track and ends up against the fence. Race control shows an off-track message.
  • A Fuel Level alarm flashes on the Porsche dash in the final seconds of one rainy clip.
  • Dust rises at the track edge while I follow a white BMW M4 GT3 in an IMSA multiclass session.
  • Chasing a green GT car all the way up the long hill in evening light.
  • Leaving the pit lane at the speed limit, then going up through the gears onto the track.
  • Quiet laps through the pine forest past the red-and-yellow kerbs, with no other car in view.
