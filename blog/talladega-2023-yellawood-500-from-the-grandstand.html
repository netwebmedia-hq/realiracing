This is the 2023 YellaWood 500, a NASCAR Cup Series race at Talladega Superspeedway in Lincoln, Alabama, USA, on October 1, 2023. Talladega is a 2.66-mile superspeedway, the longest oval in NASCAR.

What happened

The race ended in a photo finish. Ryan Blaney beat Kevin Harvick by 0.012 seconds, about a foot, and the win moved Blaney into the Round of 8.

It did not stop at the line. Harvick was disqualified in post-race inspection because the windshield fasteners were not secure. As a result, Austin Cindric got his first top-five of 2023.
