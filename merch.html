What we are testing

These designs are intentionally limited and practical. The goal is not a giant drop on day one — it is to validate which items the audience actually wants before committing to inventory, printing, and fulfillment.

  • 1. Vote DM the Instagram account or comment on the relevant launch post to choose the first item.
  • 2. Sample Pick the best concept, print a first sample, and review fit, color, and brand feel.
  • 3. Launch Open the small drop only after the design and sizing work cleanly in real life.
