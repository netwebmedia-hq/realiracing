Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

RealIRacing is a content site. There are no user accounts, no comments and no sign-up forms. This page explains the few places where your data touches third parties.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are read. It sets first-party cookies and sends usage data (page views, approximate location, device type; IP addresses are anonymized by default in GA4) to Google, under Google's own privacy policy. We do not use advertising trackers and we do not sell or share personal information.

Affiliate links

Some links to Amazon carry our Amazon Associates tag. When you click one, Amazon sets its own cookies so it can credit us with a commission on a qualifying purchase, at no extra cost to you. What Amazon collects is governed by Amazon's privacy notice. Each post that contains such links says so on the page.

Videos

YouTube videos load as a thumbnail from YouTube's image servers. The video player itself (served from youtube-nocookie.com) only loads when you press play. YouTube's own privacy policy applies from that point.

Setup packs and checkout

Buying a setup pack takes you to the checkout on netwebmedia.com, operated by the studio that builds and hosts this site. The details you enter there (such as your email address, used to deliver your pack) and the payment are handled under the NetWebMedia privacy policy. This site never sees your payment details.

Fonts and files

Typefaces and images are served from realiracing.com itself, so loading a page does not send your IP address to a font provider.

Contact by email

If you write to carlos@netwebmedia.com, the address is used only to reply to you. To ask what is held about you, or to have it deleted, send the request to the same address.

External links

Posts link to third-party sites such as retailers, manufacturers and social networks. Each has its own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Changes

If the site starts collecting anything new, this page is updated first. The date above always shows the last change.
